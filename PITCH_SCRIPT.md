# Campus Pathfinder — 2-Minute Pitch Script

**Category:** College to Career  
**Live demo:** https://namre10.github.io/campus-pathfinder/#/
**Present:** Open `pitch-deck.html` in browser (fullscreen with `F` key)

---

## Slide 1 — Hook (~15 sec)

**Visual:** 🐯 One question. Every Mizzou tool.

**Say:**
> "Mizzou students use Stellic, Handshake, MizzouOne, and housing sites — but nothing ties them together. We built **Tiger Guide**: one conversation that explains what to do and sends you to the right official tool. I'm [name], and this is **Campus Pathfinder**."

---

## Slide 2 — Problem (~15 sec)

**Visual:** Scattered portals

**Say:**
> "The problem isn't missing tools — it's **fragmentation**. A sophomore looking for an internship might need Handshake, the Career Center, and Mizzou Mentoring. A junior with a hold needs MizzouOne and Stellic. Students waste time figuring out **where to go first**."

---

## Slide 3 — Solution (~15 sec)

**Visual:** Ask → Explain → Open the right tool

**Say:**
> "**Tiger Guide** is the front door. Students ask in plain English. We answer using a curated Mizzou knowledge base — powered by Gemini — and link them to the **real** systems. We connect. We don't replace."

---

## Slide 4 — What we built (~20 sec)

**Visual:** 3 feature cards

**Say:**
> "Six modules make this real: **Tiger Guide AI** — Gemini plus a Mizzou knowledge base. A **Tools Hub** with 22 official links students actually need. A **Career bundle** — timeline, profile, mentor questions. A **Housing map** with filters, save, and compare. **Welcome intents** so new users pick a goal on day one. And **Columbia essentials** — SSN, license, and Go COMO buses. Plus Events, Community, and campus map — all frontend, demo-ready today."

---

## Slide 5 — Differentiator (~15 sec)

**Visual:** Quote + connect vs replace

**Say:**
> "Mizzou already has map.missouri.edu and offcampus.missouri.edu. We're not competing with those. Our edge is the **conversation layer** — welcome screen, pick your intent, get steps, one click to Handshake or Stellic."

---

## Slide 6 — Live demo (~15 sec)

**Visual:** Gold slide + URL

**Say:**
> "Let me show you live. I'll ask Tiger Guide about internships, open our **career timeline**, and generate mentor questions — then jump to Handshake. Everything is at **namre10.github.io/campus-pathfinder**."

**[Switch to live site if time — or stay on slide]**

---

## Slide 7 — Close (~15 sec)

**Visual:** Campus Pathfinder logo

**Say:**
> "**Campus Pathfinder**: many portals, one guide, the right action. We're building the career and obstacle layer Mizzou students don't have yet — on top of tools they already trust. Thank you — questions?"

---

## Demo checklist (if you live-demo)

1. Logo click → **welcome screen**
2. Pick **Jobs & mentors** or ask: *"Where do I find internships?"*
3. **Career → Timeline** → highlight your year
4. **Career → Mentor questions** → copy 5 questions
5. Optional: **Map → ♥ Saved** housing
